# PyPractical Design Principles

## 1\. One New Idea

Every assessment introduces one primary concept.

Everything else should reinforce previously learned material.

* * *

## 2\. Authentic Stories

Problems should resemble software that a beginner programmer could imagine writing.

Avoid artificial mathematical puzzles whenever possible.

* * *

## 3\. Natural Algorithms

Students should use loops because the story requires repetition—not because the assessment designer wanted to test loops.

* * *

## 4\. Early Success

Students should understand what the program is trying to accomplish within the first minute of reading.

* * *

## 5\. Readability Matters

Clean code is part of good programming.

Meaningful variable names are encouraged.

* * *

## 6\. Objective Grading

Every practical should be automatically gradable using unittest.

* * *

## 7\. Classroom Reality

Assessments are designed for real classrooms with real time limits.

Teacher workload is considered a design constraint.

* * *

## 8\. Equivalent Assessments

Multiple sections should receive equivalent—not identical—problems.

* * *

## 9\. Open Education

Teachers are encouraged to adapt, improve, and redistribute the materials.

* * *

## 10\. Continuous Improvement

Every classroom teaches us something.

Every assessment should evolve.
