The north star

The Manifesto.

Six beliefs shape every assessment PyPractical creates. They are the test we hold every practical against.

1Programming is learned by programming.
2Every student deserves to experience success.
3Teachers deserve tools that respect their time.
4Assessment should illuminate learning, not obscure it.
5We build for classrooms, not competitions.
6Good software engineering and good teaching share the same virtues: clarity, empathy, iteration, and craftsmanship.
“Exposure is encouraged. Dependence is not.”
“Teach the student in front of you, not the curriculum in front of you.”

See these beliefs in practice

The design principles, curriculum, and assessment families all flow from this manifesto.
